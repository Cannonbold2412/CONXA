## Opening a group now shows everything about its workflows on one screen — 2026-08-13
Before today, clicking into a group like "Sales" only showed you a short list of workflow names — to actually record, compile, review, or test one of them, you had to click through to yet another separate page. Now that separate page is gone: each workflow's row shows its whole journey right there as five buttons in a line — Record, Compile, Review, Test, and Ready to Package — like stepping stones across a stream. Each button only lights up once the step before it is done, and hovering over a greyed-out one tells you exactly what's still missing. The group's apps also moved onto this same screen, in their own column on the left, each with a small pencil to edit it and a trash can to remove it (which now asks "are you sure?" first, instead of deleting instantly). Nothing about how recording, compiling, reviewing, or testing actually works has changed — it's the same actions, just no longer scattered across two screens.
 — 2026-08-13
