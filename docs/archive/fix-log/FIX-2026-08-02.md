# Fix Log — 2026-08-02

## Fixed the login window rejecting a successful sign-in — 2026-08-02
Running an automation against a site you weren't logged into was supposed to open a browser window, let you sign in, and then continue on its own. Instead, after signing in through Google, it reported "still looks like a login page" and gave up — even though the sign-in had actually worked. The checker was looking for the words "auth," "signin," and "oauth" anywhere in the page address to decide if you were still on a login screen, but Google's own sign-in pages are full of those exact words. It was like a bouncer who turns away everyone wearing a name tag, including the staff. The checker now only looks at whether you've landed back on the actual app's website, not at every word in the address, so a real Google sign-in is correctly recognized as done. We also fixed two related problems: the login window used to sit there blocking the whole request with no time limit, so a slower sign-in could silently lose the response — it now hands back a quick "a login window is open, sign in and try again" and finishes the job in the background instead. And the window now opens using whatever session info is already saved, rather than always forcing a completely fresh login. If someone closes the login window without finishing sign-in, it reopens once automatically before giving up and asking them to try again.
